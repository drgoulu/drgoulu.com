*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Is-Emc2-dimensionally-consistent/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

Sure. SI unit for speed is [m/s], squared it is [m^2/s^2], times a mass it becomes [kg.m^2/s^2] which are [Joule](w:en:Joule), the SI unit for energy.

Notice that the unit of speed squared [m^2/s^2] is also known as the [Gray](w:en:Gray_(unit)), which is used for [Absorbed dose](w:en:Absorbed_dose) of radiation. Nice coïncidence, isn’t it ?
