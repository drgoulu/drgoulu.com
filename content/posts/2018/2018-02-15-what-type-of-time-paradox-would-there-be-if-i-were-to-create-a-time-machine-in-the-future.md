It’s called the [Grandfather paradox - Wikipedia](w:en:Grandfather_paradox) , however IMHO there should be a different name for 2 different things :

- when a travel in the past makes the travel impossible in the future, as in most cases
- when the travel is mandatory for the travel to be possible in the future, as in your case and in Back to the Future III, when Marty shows Doc a working “flux capacitor” before he built it.
