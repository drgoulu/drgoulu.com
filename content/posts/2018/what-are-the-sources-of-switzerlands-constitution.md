Technically, the Swiss constitution is changed at each successful referendum, so about once a year. From time to time it is rewritten to modernité its phrasing or delete old stuff.

So the current main revision is from 1999.

See [Swiss Federal Constitution - Wikipedia](w:en:Swiss_Federal_Constitution)
