*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Are-black-holes-revolving-around-something-If-so-at-what-speed/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

Yes, [Binary black holes](w:en:Binary_black_hole) revolve around each other at fantastic speed until they merge. In [PKS 1302-102](w:en:PKS_1302-102), the lighter black hole has been [clocked at seven percent light speed](https://arstechnica.com/science/2015/09/supermassive-black-holes-found-spiraling-in-at-seven-percent-light-speed/) !

But my favourite is [OJ 287](w:en:OJ_287) : a [supermassive black hole](w:en:Supermassive_black_hole) of 100 **m**illion solar masses is orbiting one another of 18 **b**illion solar masses in about 12 years, crossing the [accretion disk](w:en:Accretion_disk) twice, which produces flashes that could be seen by telescopes on Earth since more than a century.

In about 10′000 years they will merge in a festival of gravitational waves.
