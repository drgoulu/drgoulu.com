*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Is-there-any-policy-in-Switzerland-that-provides-free-1000-euros-per-month-to-a-Swiss-person/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

No. First, Switzerland’s currency is the Swiss Franc (CHF), not the Euro ;-)

Second, in 2016, the [Swiss referendum for a basic income](w:en:Swiss_referendums,_2016) was rejected by all Cantons and 77% of the voters.

Since Switzerland has no [Sovereign wealth fund](w:en:Sovereign_wealth_fund), this money would come from taxes, so what’s the point in paying 12000 CHF per year more taxes to get CHF 1000 per month from the state ???

1175 CHF / month is the minimal pension you get when retired (AVS). Hopefully you’ll get more through pension funds.
