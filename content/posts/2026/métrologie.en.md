Throughout my nearly 50-year career in computer science, I witnessed extraordinary technological leaps. But reflecting on what fundamentally advanced the most, I would argue it is the science of measurement: [metrology](w:Metrology). For everyday users, it seems more like an engineering discipline that progressed steadily, rather than spectacularly.

From a scientific standpoint, however, we have learned to measure unfathomable things with staggering precision, such as:

- The [age of the universe](w:Age_of_the_universe) with astonishing accuracy: $13.77 \pm 0.040$ billion years, or within 0.3%
- The average ocean temperature 100,000 years ago to within 1°C
- The exact felling year of any piece of wood dating back to 7,000 BC
