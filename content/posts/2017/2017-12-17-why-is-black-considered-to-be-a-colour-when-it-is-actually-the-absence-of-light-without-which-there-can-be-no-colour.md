*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Why-is-black-considered-to-be-a-colour-when-it-is-actually-the-absence-of-light-without-which-there-can-be-no-colour/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

Because you can buy black ink or paint. I mean, from a painter or industrial point of view, black is a color because you use it just like any other color.

Of course, from the physics point of view it’s not a color, but since “black” objects are usually not as dark as [Vantablack](w:en:Vantablack), we need a name for “very dark”.
