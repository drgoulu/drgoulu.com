*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/If-mass-can-be-converted-to-energy-why-does-the-universe-contain-both/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

[Energy](w:en:Energy) is not a “thing”, it’s a measure, a value, a property of things. So when you convert mass to energy in an atomic bomb for example, the energy is in the kinetic energy of ejected particles, and in the photons emitted.

Conversely, when you collision particles in an accelerator, you may produce particles more massive than those that collided because of the added kinetic energy. [Top quark](w:en:Top_quark)s for example.

Apparently, the Universe produced mass from very energetic massless particles very early in the BigBang, and since then stars rather convert mass into energy.

But one of the most interesting question is why the BigBang didn’t produce as much antimatter as matter, which could have mutually annihilated to produce an Universe full of photons. The answer is : we don’t know (yet).
