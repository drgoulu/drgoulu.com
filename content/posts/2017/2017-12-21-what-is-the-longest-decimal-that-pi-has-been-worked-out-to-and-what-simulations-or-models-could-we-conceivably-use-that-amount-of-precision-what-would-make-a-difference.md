I asked a related question on StackeExchange some time ago : [How many digits of Pi are required in physics?](https://physics.stackexchange.com/questions/9621/how-many-digits-of-pi-are-required-in-physics/9631) and revieved an interesting answer from Lubos Motl mentioning the [Anomalous magnetic dipole moment](w:en:Anomalous_magnetic_dipole_moment) as an experiment for which about ten decimals of pi are required. As he said “So in practice, π≈3.141592654 would be OK everywhere in the part of physics that is testable”
