[Planetary equilibrium temperature - Wikipedia](w:en:Planetary_equilibrium_temperature) gives you the answer.

it’s 206K for Mars, 255 K for Earth, and 307 K for Venus.

So Earth would be about 50 K (or C) hotter on Venus orbit, and 50K cooler on Mars orbit.

The climate would be so different I think nobody can really say what would be the max and mins, but I guess only Antarctica would be livable for us on Venus orbit, and Earth would be completely frozen, oceans included, on Mars orbit.
