[l'Autriche](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/fr/stakeholder-relations/states/autriche), [la Belgique](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/fr/stakeholder-relations/states/belgique), Finlande](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/fr/stakeholder-relations/states/finlande), [la France](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/fr/stakeholder-relations/states/france), [la Grèce](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/fr/stakeholder-relations/states/grece), [la Hongrie](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/fr/stakeholder-relations/states/hongrie), [Israël](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/fr/stakeholder-relations/states/israel), [l'Italie](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/fr/stakeholder-relations/states/italie), [la Norvège](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/fr/stakeholder-relations/states/norvege), [les Pays-Bas](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/fr/stakeholder-relations/states/pays-bas), [la Royaume-Uni](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/fr/stakeholder-relations/states/royaume-uni), [la Serbie](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/fr/stakeholder-relations/states/serbie), >
> [Chypre](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/cyprus) et la [Slovénie](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/slovenia) sont États membres associés en phase préalable à l'adhésion. [La Croatie](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/croatia), [l'Inde](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/india), [la Lituanie](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/lithuania), [le Pakistan](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/pakistan), [la Turquie](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/turkey) et [l'Ukraine](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/ukraine) sont États membres associés.
>
>
>
> [Les États-Unis d'Amérique](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/United-States-America), [la Fédération de Russie](https://web.archive.org/web/20201123213528/https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/Russia), [le Japon](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/japan), [l'Union européenne](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/european-union), [l'Institut unifié de recherche nucléaire (JINR)](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/jinr) et [l'UNESCO](https://international-relations.web.cern.ch/stakeholder-relations/states/unesco) ont actuellement le statut d'observateur.
