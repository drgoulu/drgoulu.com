*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/What-are-the-factors-enabling-the-Swiss-people-to-produce-luxurious-watches/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

History, tradition, marketing, marketing and marketing.

- History : around 1700 many french protestants ([Huguenots - Wikipedia](w:en:Huguenots) ) fled to french-speaking Switzerland, especially Geneva-the-calvinist [[1]](#KRBkn) . They brought their knowledge of watchmaking with them …
- Tradition : watchmaking was the perfect winter job for people living in the Jura mountains. In summer they were peasants, in winter they manufactured watch parts. They became very skilled at this and many companies in this area are very famous for high precision / high quality micro systems like medical devices, scientific instruments, components of space probes and satellites
- Marketing 1 : “Swiss Precision” became a reference, written on all mechanical watches before the 1970’s
- Marketing 2: after the quartz revolution, there was no way to be more precise than cheap quartz watches with mechanical movements.
