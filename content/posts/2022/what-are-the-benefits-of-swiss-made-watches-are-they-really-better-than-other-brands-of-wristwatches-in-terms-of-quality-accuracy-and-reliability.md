You don’t wear a (mechanical) wristwatch for its quality, accuracy or reliability. You wear it because it’s a beautiful piece of work, a jewel, a luxury, a collector’s object, even an investment.

If you just want to know the time, look at your phone.

Same for cars : to go from A to B, a Toyota is fine. But the car you really want is a Ferrari. Or at least a Mercedes.

Swiss made watches are mostly in the Mercedes-Ferrari range. Toyotas are japanese, or chinese.
