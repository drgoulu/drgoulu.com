*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/What-kind-of-boat-was-Noahs-ark/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

read somewhere it should have been 10x larger than the [Seawise Giant](w:en:Seawise_Giant) only to keep a couple of each animal species, definitely not enough to allow them to spread afterwards (it is estimated that a species of mammals gets extinct if its population goes below about 500).

And it should have been quite fast to pick up endemic species from all around the world in a few months, from Antarctica to Alaska, and from Australia to Galapagos …

So I’d say it was a fleet of about 2500 supertankers on hydrofoils, with nuclear propulsion.

And another event larger fleet for plants.
