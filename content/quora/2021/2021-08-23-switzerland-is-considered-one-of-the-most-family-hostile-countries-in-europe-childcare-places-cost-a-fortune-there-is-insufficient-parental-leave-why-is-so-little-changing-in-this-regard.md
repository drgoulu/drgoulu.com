because it’s a direct democracy, and the majority is (globally) right wing, so social subsidies are not easily accepted in referendums.

Many people (including me) tend to think that having children is not mandatory : if you have children, you should support their (heavy) costs. The swiss social welfare is designed to help people in trouble, not to subsidize families who can afford paying the cost of raising children.

Same for parental leave : who pays ? Some employers might, for example to attract young skilled women from abroad. But why should it be paid by taxes of people who supported the costs of raising their own children ?

BTW, the cost of childcare is “a fortune” because people working in kindergarten also want a good salary[[1]](#DRxpe) .
