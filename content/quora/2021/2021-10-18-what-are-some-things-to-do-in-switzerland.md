*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/What-are-some-things-to-do-in-Switzerland/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

1. Travel by train. You can go anywhere quickly and on time, and see beautiful places. There are fantastic “touristic” trains like Jungfraujoch, Gornergrat, Bernina, “Glacier Express”, “GoldenPass”, or Brienzrothorn. Go to one of them, preferably on a sunny day!
2. Visit a real city, one where people really live like Bern, Zürich, Basel, Lausanne … Luzern or Interlaken are nice, but you’ll only see tourists.
