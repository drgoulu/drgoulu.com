*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/My-wife-and-I-are-looking-to-move-to-Switzerland-and-hope-to-become-permanent-residents-citizenship-What-was-your-road-to-citizenship/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

For me the road was 9 months long : I was born Switzerland from swiss parents.

The quickest way in your situation is to divorce. Then each of you marry a swiss citizen. Stay married for 5 years and apply to [Swiss citizenship](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/integration-einbuergerung/schweizer-werden/verheiratet.html) . Divorce and remarry your current wife.

It’s half the time needed for [ordinary naturalisation](https://www.sem.admin.ch/sem/en/home/integration-einbuergerung/schweizer-werden/ordentlich.html) …
