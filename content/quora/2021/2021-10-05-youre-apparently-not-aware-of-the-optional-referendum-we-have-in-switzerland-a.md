You’re apparently not aware of the [Optional referendum](w:en:Optional_referendum) we have in Switzerland. Any decision of the elected representatives can be challenged.

The system is not perfect, and actually we have a “semi-direct” democracy rather than a direct at Federal level and in most Cantons. But some Cantons still have [Landsgemeinde](w:en:Landsgemeinde), and I think e-voting could be a way to e-Landsgemeinde…
