[Alejandro Jenkins's answer to What is the relative rate of time passage at the center of the sun compared to the surface of earth?](https://www.quora.com/What-is-the-relative-rate-of-time-passage-at-the-center-of-the-sun-compared-to-the-surface-of-earth/answer/Alejandro-Jenkins?ch=10&oid=8910608&share=c84fe76b&srid=pzDv&target_type=answer)
