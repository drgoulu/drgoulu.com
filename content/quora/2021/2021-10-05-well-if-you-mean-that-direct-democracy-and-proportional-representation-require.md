well, If you mean that direct democracy and proportional representation require educated citizens, I agree.

However, votes are inherently irrational. If a topic can be rationaly analyzed, a vote is useless : everybody agrees to stop on red light. I often say to my daughters “if you don’t know what to vote, vote with your guts, but vote because elderly vote massively and decide your future if you don’t.”
