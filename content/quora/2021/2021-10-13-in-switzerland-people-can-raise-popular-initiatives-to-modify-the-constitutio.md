*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Why-does-Switzerland-still-have-forced-conscription/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

in Switzerland, people can raise “[Popular initiatives](w:en:Popular_initiative_(Switzerland))” to modify the Constitution. A referendum is organized to accept the modification, or not.

[The text of this one (in french here)](https://www.bk.admin.ch/ch/f/pore/vi/vis391t.html) proposed to replace [Art 59 of the Constitution (in English here](https://www.fedlex.admin.ch/eli/cc/1999/404/en#art_59) ) by a voluntary civil service.

This proposal was rejected by 73% (so the compulsory military service remains), while 27% thought the voluntary civil service was a “viable alternative”.

it is a “failure” for those who organized the popular initiative. Of course they didn’t reach 50%, but even worse they didn’t reach 40%, which would be a political signal meaning “there’s something important here”. And not even 30% meaning “this is a topic”. With 27%, the subject if now “out of the political scope” for decades.
