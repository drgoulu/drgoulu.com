*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Which-part-of-Switzerland-is-safest-with-the-least-crime/answer/Alessandro-Fuga-1)*

Alas, you pictured probably the most deadly place in Switzerland.

Matterhorn is one of the [World's 10 Most Dangerous Mountains](https://gearjunkie.com/climbing/mountaineering/worlds-10-most-dangerous-mountains), with about 12 persons killed every year, twice the number of murders in our largest city, Zürich.

Besides, there is a long standing controversy about the accident that occurred during the [First ascent of the Matterhorn.](w:en:First_ascent_of_the_Matterhorn) Some believe a guide cut the rope between him and Lord Francis Douglas to save his life…

So please don’t go there unless you are a trained mountaineer, with a good guide and a good rope.
