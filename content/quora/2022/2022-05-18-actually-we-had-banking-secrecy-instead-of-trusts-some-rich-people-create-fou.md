*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Are-most-Swiss-employed-or-are-they-living-out-of-trust-funds/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

actually we had banking secrecy instead of trusts ;-)

Some rich people create [Foundations](w:en:Foundation_(nonprofit)) for non-profit or charity and live on managing it, but certainly not 3%

I guess your 3% are mostly retired people who saved their money on private trust funds. In Switzerland we have a mixed state/private [Pension system](w:en:Pension_system_in_Switzerland) that is a good comprise as we like them here.

From what I see here ( [Retirement provision in Switzerland: What's the situation with the pension system?](https://www.credit-suisse.com/ch/en/articles/private-banking/altersvorsorge-in-der-schweiz-das-wichtigste-auf-einen-blick-202204.html) ) about a third of swiss taxpayers (i.e. almost all citizens) have no private “third pillar”, so their retirement depends only on social (first pillar) and mandatory (second pillar) provisions.

Said otherwise, 66% of swiss people have some money on a private pension fund.
