*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Does-Switzerland-have-any-free-places-for-travelers-to-stay/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

Very few.

You are allowed to camp in mountains, above the forests limit (~2200m), outside natural reserves, and just for one night : you must take your tent down and move along before camping again.
