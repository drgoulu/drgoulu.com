*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Why-do-you-think-America-is-the-best-country-in-the-world/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

America is not a country, it’s a continent with 35 countries on it.

I assume you mean one of them called “United States of America”. It ranks :

- 2nd by [military expenditure per capita](w:en:List_of_countries_by_military_expenditure_per_capita) (sorry, Israel is first)
- 3rd by population
- 13th by [GDP (PPP) per capita](w:en:List_of_countries_by_GDP_(PPP)_per_capita)according to the CIA
- 15th by [Human Development Index](w:en:Human_Development_Index)
- 17th by [carbon dioxide emissions per capita](w:en:List_of_countries_by_carbon_dioxide_emissions_per_capita)
- 25th by [Democracy Index](w:en:Democracy_Index)
- 38th by [Life expectancy](w:en:List_of_countries_by_life_expectancy)

but true, it ranks first by:

- total GDP (who cares ?)
- [total military expenditures](w:en:List_of_countries_by_military_expenditures) (many care…)
- [health expenditure per capita](w:en:List_of_countries_by_total_health_expenditure_per_capita) (to pay for the 38th life expectancy…)
- [incarceration rate](w:en:List_of_countries_by_incarceration_rate)
- and probably several other criteria

I think USA were the greatest country on Earth possibly
