[Federal Supreme Court of Switzerland - Wikipedia](w:en:Federal_Supreme_Court_of_Switzerland) ??? It has invalided some popular initiatives, which are modifications of the Constitution issued by the ultimate legislative power in Switzerland : the people.

The European Court on Human Rights is a court we decided to accept as one more supreme court for some special cases, like La Haye or [the International Criminal Court](w:en:United_States_and_the_International_Criminal_Court) …

Well, if I have to choose between a “[Rechtsstaat”](w:en:Rechtsstaat)where a few politically elected judges can overrule the majority of people and the (semi-) direct democracy we have, I’d chose the (semi-)direct democracy. EU chose the former, which is precisely my point.
