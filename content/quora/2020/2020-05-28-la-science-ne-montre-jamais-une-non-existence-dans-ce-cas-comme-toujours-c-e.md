*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://fr.quora.com/Existe-t-il-une-particule-hypothétique-dont-les-physiciens-sont-sûrs-quelle-existe-mais-qui-na-pas-pu-être-prouvé-expérimentalement/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

La science ne montre jamais une "non existence". Dans ce cas comme toujours, c'est à ceux qui prétendent que quelque chose existe de le montrer de manière reproductible.

Dans le cas présent : ([X17 particle -](w:en:X17_particle)Wikipedia)

> As of December 2019, the ATOMKI paper describing the particle has not been [peer reviewed](w:en:Peer_review) and should therefore be considered preliminary.
>
>
>
> Furthermore, efforts by [CERN](w:en:CERN) and other groups to independently detect the particle have been unsuccessful.
>
>
>
> The ATOMKI group had claimed to find various other new particles earlier in 2016 but abandoned these claims later, without an explanation of what caused the spurious signals. The group has also been accused of [cherry-picking](w:en:Cherry-picking) results that support new particles while discarding [null results](w:en:Null_result).
>
>
>
> The X17 particle is not consistent with the [Standard Model](w:en:Standard_Model), so its existence would need to be explained by another theory.
