*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/What-is-life-in-Switzerland-like/answers/13555926)*

For one thing, Switzerland has only 8 million inhabitants, so our President is as important as the Mayor of a large city.

And besides, there is probably security around. You don't see them, they are very discrete but we don't want something like the assassination of Olof Palme to happen here.
