*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Why-do-some-people-believe-time-travel-is-possible/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

Because they learned about [CPT symmetry](w:en:CPT_symmetry), [Closed timelike curves](w:en:Closed_timelike_curve) and might have read [How to Build a Time Machine](w:en:How_to_Build_a_Time_Machine), so they know it might be possible.

I think this century will see lab scale experiments (on particles) that show if the Universe allows backwards time travel or not.

Until then, the question is open, do why do some people believe time travel is NOT possible ?
