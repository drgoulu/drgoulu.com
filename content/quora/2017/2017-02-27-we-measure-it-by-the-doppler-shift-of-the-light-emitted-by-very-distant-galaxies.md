*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Why-dont-we-expand-with-the-Universe-if-our-atoms-are-mostly-empty-space/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

we measure it by the doppler shift of the light emitted by very distant galaxies. If a galaxy is 1 megaparsec away (about 3.3 million light years, which is close), it seems to receed at 67 km/s from us, and its light is “redshifted” by 67/300000 =0.00022 which we can measure.

I think we measure doppler shifts even 10000 lower than that for exoplanet detection, but it would require a billion times more to detect the redshift of your feet compared to your head…
