*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Are-Switzerland-elections-and-referendums-rigged/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

Definitely not. Or at least not significantly, I’ll explain below.

I was a member of the Central Electoral Commission of the Canton of Geneva [[1]](#sARYU) as an independent (= no party) expert for the internet voting system. Apart from another expert in voting laws, all members are members of the various parties, ranging from “communists” to the rightmost convervators. And we were all working together at least 4 week-ends per year to check the organisation of the polls and verify the counting process.

It was a very interesting experience for me to see democracy in action, with state officials really devoted to the task and promptly providing us with any information of document we required.

The more I think about why it works here and not there, the more I’m convinced [Proportional representation](w:en:Proportional_representation)is the key. We have it at all political levels.
