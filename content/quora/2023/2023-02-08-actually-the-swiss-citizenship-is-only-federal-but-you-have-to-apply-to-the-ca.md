*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/How-long-exactly-does-it-take-to-get-a-citizenship-in-Switzerland/answer/Wolfgang-Maleck)*

Actually, the swiss citizenship is only federal, but you have to apply to the canton or commune where you live, who might have some requirements such as mastering the language.

In some cantons, you might have some political rights (vote, elect, be elected) at the cantonal or communal level before you get the swiss citizenship.
