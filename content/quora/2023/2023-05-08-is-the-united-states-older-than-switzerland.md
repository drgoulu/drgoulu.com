Depends what you count.

- 1291 Swiss [Federal Charter of 1291](w:en:Federal_Charter_of_1291)between 3 original cantons
- 1520–1607 : discovery of the East Coast from Florida to Labrador by French, English and Spanish explorers
- 1776 : declaration of US independence
- 1783 : end of US independence war and actual independence
- 1848 : Swiss Constitution of a Federal state
