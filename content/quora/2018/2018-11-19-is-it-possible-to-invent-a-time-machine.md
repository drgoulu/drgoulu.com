Read [How to Build a Time Machine](w:en:How_to_Build_a_Time_Machine) by Paul Davies, who thinks it is, or might be possible in some millenia.

I think it is very important to try building small scale experiment to know IF the universe allows time travel or not, which will tell wether [Presentists](w:en:Philosophical_presentism) or [Eternalists](w:en:Eternalism_(philosophy_of_time)) are correct.
