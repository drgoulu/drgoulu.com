*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Is-a-dream-like-a-time-machine/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

Our brain is indeed a “virtual time machine”, in the sense that we can explore possible futures and reconstruct the past, to the point that the brain makes little difference between those activities . Read [Remembering the Past is Like Imagining the Future - Cosmic Variance](http://blogs.discovermagazine.com/cosmicvariance/2009/04/14/remembering-the-past-is-like-imagining-the-future/#.W0XIDEBuZ5E)

Research has shown that this activity occurs also during dreaming, especially that people are more able to predict the future after a nap[[1]](#ofYEx) . See [How dreams predict the future by making sense of the past – Sue Llewellyn | Aeon Essays](https://aeon.co/essays/how-dreams-predict-the-future-by-making-sense-of-the-past)
