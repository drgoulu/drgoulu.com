*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/What-citizens-of-a-country-rarely-complains-about-its-government/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

[A country](w:en:Direct_democracy) where citizens keep control over the government, where almost all important decisions are subject to referendum, where citizens can change the Constitution, and where the government itself is proportionally elected, therefore representing (almost) all political parties.

In such a country, you can’t really complain about the government, you have to complain about other citizens, or yourself …

(My dear american friends, [we were so close](http://www.theswisscenter.org/sister-republics), what have you done ??? )
