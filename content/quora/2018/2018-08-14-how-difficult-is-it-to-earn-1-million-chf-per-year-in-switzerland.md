*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/How-difficult-is-it-to-earn-1-million-CHF-per-year-in-Switzerland/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

As an employee, it’s almost impossible[[1]](#nXedl) . Only a few top managers in large multinational companies (and they are not so many) are paid above 1 MCHF

As a very good medical doctor, specialist in a private clinic or a very experimented lawyer in a law firm working with financial institutions, maybe…

As an investor, pretty easily if you have 10–100 millions to invest ;-)
