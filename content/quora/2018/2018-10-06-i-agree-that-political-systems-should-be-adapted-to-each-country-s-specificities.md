*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/How-important-is-living-in-a-democracy-to-most-Americans-who-often-dont-vote-and-dont-seem-to-care-that-their-president-is-working-at-becoming-a-dictator/answer/Bob-Jones-1151)*

I agree that political systems should be adapted to each country's specificities. Switzerland is indeed a semi direct democracy : representatives are elected, but each of their decisions may be challenged by a referendum.

We had direct democracy at the local level ([Landsgemeinde](w:en:Landsgemeinde)) but we had to drop it when population rose, especially when women were allowed to vote… BTW if didn't become a “dictatorship of the majority” otherwise men wouldn't have shared their power…

So right, size is a problem, at least at federal level for USA, but I'm pretty sure it might be applied at local level, especially with Internet voting.
