*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/What-is-the-functions-of-fox01/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

La pizza était **si**bonne **que**j’en ai repris : The pizza was **so**good **that**I took some more.

J’ai **tellement/tant** aimé cette pizza **que**j’en ai repris : I liked this pizza **so much that**I took some more.

J’ai mangé **tellement/tant** de pizza **que**je suis malade : I ate **so much** pizza **that**I’m sick

“tant” and “tellement” are synonyms[[1]](#OnNQX) , or almost. I can’t think of a case where one would be correct and the other wouldn’t.
