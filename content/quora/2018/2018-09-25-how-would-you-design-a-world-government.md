*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/How-would-you-design-a-world-government/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

I wouldn’t. Decentralized governments adapted to local cultures and problems are much more efficient.

To solve international problems, we already have the [United Nations](w:en:United_Nations). It should be reformed to become more efficient:

1. by supressing the [veto power](w:en:United_Nations_Security_Council_veto_power) of the 5 permanent members of the Security Council, which are actually the winners of WW2, not reprenetents
2. by becoming [Bicameral](w:en:Bicameralism), with a “Senate” representing each State like now, and a “Congress” proportional the the population of each State.
3. by NOT increasing its power. Applying the current rules to ALL member states and their leaders would be well enough to me.
4. by installing it in a single location, preferably a small neutral country in the middle of Europe, in a city with a beautiful water jet :-)
