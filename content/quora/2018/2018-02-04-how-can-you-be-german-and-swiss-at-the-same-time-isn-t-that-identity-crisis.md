*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/How-can-you-be-German-and-Swiss-at-the-same-time-isnt-that-identity-crisis/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

I guess you mixed up nationality and language. [Swiss German](w:en:Swiss_German) is a language spoken in Switzerland, and by extension the people who speak that language. They are definitely swiss, with their own culture which is quite different from the German language and culture.

BTW the “Swiss french” is also somewhat different from the French language, and swiss people who speak French don't consider themselves French at all. Same for “Swiss Italians”.

I don't think Swiss people have any identity crisis with respect to their neighbours. We might rather have some kind of superiority complex.
