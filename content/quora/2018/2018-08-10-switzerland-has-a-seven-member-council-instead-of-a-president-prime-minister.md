*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/What-are-some-countries-which-use-a-unique-form-of-government-not-used-by-many-others/answer/Michael-Wald-5)*

“Switzerland has a seven-member Council instead of a president/prime minister.” Not exactly. The [Federal Council](w:en:Federal_Council_(Switzerland)) is the full government, with 7 “ministers”, each one being “President” for one year in turn.

The originality of this is rather that the 7 are de facto proportionally elected : we have socialistes, nationalists, and member of 2 other parties in between in the same (stable) government !
