By the way, since you mention Dutch you might have noticed it sounds close to “Deutsch” which means German in German. Dutch is a germanic language like Allemanisch (aka Swiss German) and I think it has about the same “level of difference” as Dutch with respect to modern German. But I can assure you people from the Netherlands don’t feel German at all…
