*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://fr.quora.com/Est-ce-que-le-temps-est-la-seule-chose-infinie/answer/Pierre-Goffinet)*

Oui effectivement d'après [Two Things Are Infinite: the Universe and Human Stupidity](https://quoteinvestigator.com/2010/05/04/universe-einstein/) ça vient effectivement de Perls. Dans “Ego, Hunger, and Aggression: a Revision of Freud’s Theory and Method.” il y avait :

> As modern times promote hasty eating to a large extent, it is not surprising to learn that a great astronomer said: “Two things are infinite, as far as we know – the universe and human stupidity.” To-day we know that this statement is not quite correct. **Einstein has proved that the universe is limited.**
