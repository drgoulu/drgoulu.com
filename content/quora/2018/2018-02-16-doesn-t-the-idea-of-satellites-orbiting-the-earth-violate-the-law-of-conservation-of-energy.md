*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Doesn-t-the-idea-of-satellites-orbiting-the-Earth-violate-the-law-of-conservation-of-energy-Wouldn-t-they-over-heat-from-the-sun-hitting-the-solar-panels-Having-no-way-to-dissipate-the-heat/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

The conservation of energy applies to a closed, or isolated system. A satellite exposed to sunlight is not a closed system. It will stabilize at the temperature where it radiates in thermal infrared the same power it recieves from the Sun.
