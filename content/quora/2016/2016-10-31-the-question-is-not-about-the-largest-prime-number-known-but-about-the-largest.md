the question is not about the largest prime number known, but about the largest number…

G is not prime as it is constructed by powers. There are (an infinity of) primes larger than G, but we don't know tjem. There is no way to test the primality of numbers as large as G.

The largest known prime with its millions of digits is incredibly smaller than G.
