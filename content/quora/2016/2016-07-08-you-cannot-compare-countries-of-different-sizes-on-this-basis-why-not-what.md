*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Why-is-Switzerland-so-much-more-successful-than-the-rest-of-Europe-EU/answer/Granville-Phillips)*

“you cannot compare countries of different sizes on this basis”. Why not ? What prevents every area of about 8M people in larger countries to develop niches ? London City or Manhattan are banking niches too. Switzerland also has a chocolate niche and a watch niche, Silicon valley has a software niche, everyone can build niches. The thing is to actually build them.
