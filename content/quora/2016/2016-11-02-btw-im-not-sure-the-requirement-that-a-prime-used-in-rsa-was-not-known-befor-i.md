BTW, I’m not sure the requirement that a prime used in RSA was not known befor is relevant since there are enough different primes of 512 bits (154 decimal digits) : about 0.3% of them are prime. The probability that, starting from a random bumber of 154 bits and incrementing, you find the same prime twice is close to zero.
