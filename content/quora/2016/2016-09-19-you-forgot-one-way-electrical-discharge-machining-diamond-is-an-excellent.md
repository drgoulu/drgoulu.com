*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/If-the-only-way-to-cut-a-diamond-is-with-another-diamond-how-was-the-cutting-diamond-cut-to-be-used-for-cutting/answer/David-Kahana)*

You forgot one way : [Electrical discharge machining](w:en:Electrical_discharge_machining) . Diamond is an (excellent) electric conductor, so it can be “cut” with a wire generating sparks. This is done industrially to reshape grinding mills containing diamond particles.
