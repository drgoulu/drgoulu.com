oui

100km/h = 27,7778 m/s

en 10 s : 27.777/10 = 2.77 m/s^2 = 2.77/9.81 = 0.28 g

en 5 s : 27.777/5 = 5.55 m/s^2 = 5.55/9.81 = 0.56 g
