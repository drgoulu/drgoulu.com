Les 5 journaux scientifiques les plus sérieux sur le climat sont[[1]](#kMZzR) :

1. [Bulletin of the American Meteorological Society (BAMS)](https://www.ametsoc.org/index.cfm/ams/publications/bulletin-of-the-american-meteorological-society-bams/)
2. [Journal of the Meteorological Society of Japan](http://jmsj.metsoc.jp/) (en libre accès)
3. [WIREs Climate Change](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/17577799)
4. [Journal of Climate](https://www.ametsoc.org/index.cfm/ams/publications/journals/journal-of-climate/)
5. [Global Biogeochemical Cycles](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/journal/19449224)
6. j'ajoute [Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics](https://www.atmospheric-chemistry-and-physics.net/) parce qu'il est européen et en libre accès
