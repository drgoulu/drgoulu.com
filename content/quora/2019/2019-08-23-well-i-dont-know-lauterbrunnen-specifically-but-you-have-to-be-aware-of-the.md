*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://www.quora.com/Is-it-a-good-idea-to-buy-a-house-in-Lauterbrunnen-to-spend-some-time-there-without-facing-unfriendly-looks-from-locals-being-not-Swiss/answer/Dr-Goulu)*

Well, I don’t know Lauterbrunnen specifically, but you have to be aware of the (high) cost of living in Switzerland, especially in touristic places. Check [Property for sale in Lauterbrunnen](https://en.comparis.ch/immobilien/marktplatz/lauterbrunnen/kaufen) to have an idea.

PLUS : selling to foreigners is limited to 1500 homes/year for whole Switzerland (“Lex Kohler”) and selling for secondary home (=if you don’t live there …) is forbidden in places with over 20% of secondary homes (“Lex Weber”), which is the case in Berner Oberland. So believe me : the hardest is to find a home that you are allowed to buy (and can afford.) Renting is much less of a problem.

Switzerland is a small country, or rather a wide city : you have pretty much the same facilities everywhere. You can go anywhere by train, so Lauterbrunnen is 20 mins from Interlaken, 1h20 from Bern and 3h from Zürich airport.
