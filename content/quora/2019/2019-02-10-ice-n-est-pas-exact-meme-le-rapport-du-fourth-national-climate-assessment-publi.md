*Réponse publiée [sur Quora](https://fr.quora.com/Pourquoi-les-Américains-ne-croient-ils-pas-que-le-changement-climatique-est-une-réalité/answer/Jean-Bossaert)*

ICe n'est pas exact. Même le rapport du [Fourth National Climate Assessment](w:en:Fourth_National_Climate_Assessment) publié en 2017 sous Trump dit clairement :

> it is extremely likely that human activities, especially emissions of greenhouse gases, are the dominant cause of the observed warming since the mid-20th century. For the warming over the last century, there is no convincing alternative explanation supported by the extent of the observational evidence

La presse générale n'est en effet pas une source fiable, alors lisez des journaux scientifiques comme ceux de l'American Meteorological Society (AMETSOC) qui sont parmi les plus réputés au monde sur le sujet. Leur [Climate Change](https://www.ametsoc.org/ams/index.cfm/about-ams/ams-statements/statements-of-the-ams-in-force/climate-change/) Statement dit :

> Warming of the climate system now is unequivocal, according to many different kinds of evidence.
>
>
>
> …
>
>
>
> It is clear from extensive scientific evidence that the dominant cause of the rapid change in climate of the past half century is human-induced increases in the amount of atmospheric greenhouse gases, including carbon dioxide (CO2), chlorofluorocarbons, methane, and nitrous oxide. The most important of these over the long term is CO2, whose concentration in the atmosphere is rising principally as a result of fossil-fuel combustion and deforestation.

A un certain moment, le scepticisme scientifique devient de la mauvaise foi…
